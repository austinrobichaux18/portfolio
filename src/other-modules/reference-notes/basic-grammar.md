# Basic Japanese Grammar — Seed Notes

Raw reference notes for a future "Basic Japanese grammar" module (see
`../TODO.md`). Captured as given, not yet built into anything or verified for
completeness — treat as a starting point, not a finished reference.

## Parts of speech

- **Noun**: person, place, thing (いずみ、うち、いえ、ねこ、たべもの)
- **Verb**: action (あるく、たべる、ねる)
- **Adjective**: describing word (あかい、たかい、やすい)

## Particles

- **は**: non-logical. Flags the topic of the sentence ("As for X...")
- **が**: topic of sentence
- **を**: object of sentence — describes what is being verbed/adjectived
- **に**: target of sentence — describes the target/thing/person/object that is
  being verbed/adjectived
- **の**: possessive — describes ownership. A の B → A's B
- **と**: and
- **も**: too, also

## Sentence enders

- **だ / です**: used after nouns, for an "A is B" sentence
- **い**: used after adjectives, for an "A is B" sentence
- **う**: used after verbs, for an "A does B" sentence

## て form

- **Verbs**: て form chart as usual
- **Adjectives**: replace い with く, then add て

## Past tense

- **Adjectives**: replace い with かった
- **Verbs**: て form conversion, with た instead of て

## Nouns/Verbs → Adjectives

**Nouns** (some take な, some take の):
- **な**: replaces だ when making an adjectivable noun
  (いぬがやんちゃだ → やんちゃないぬがいる)
- **の**: shows belonging to a class
  (ぴんくいろ is "pink color" as a noun; ぴんくいろのどれす is "pink color's
  dress", i.e. a dress belonging to the class "pink")

## Adjectives → Nouns

Use the い stem. (まわる = "go around" verb → まわり = noun form, "surroundings")

## Self Move / Other Move

- **Self Move**: ends with る (まわる — go around)
- **Other Move**: ends with す (まわす — send/make go around)

## Absolute and Relative Time Expressions

- **Relative**: front of sentence, no particle (あした わたしが あるく)
- **Absolute**: front of sentence, with に particle (さんじに わたしが あるく)

## Negative

- **Verbs**: swap to あ stem (ichidan verbs just drop る), then add ない
- **Adjectives**: replace い with く, then add ない
- **Nouns**: replace だ with では, then add ない
  (これは、ぺんだ → これは、ぺんではない)
- **Past tense**: ない → なかった
- **Exception**: ます → ません

## "Helper verbs"

- **たい**: い stem + たい — want
- **ください**: prefixing word needs て form (またあそんでください)
- **てみる**: "try and see", formal (たべてみる — "try and eat"/"eat and see";
  やってみる/してみる — やる＝する, "do and see"/"give it a try")

## Formal

- **Verbs**: い stem + ます

## Verb stem swaps

| Stem | Attaches | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| い | ます | formal |
| い | たい | want |
| い | そう | seems like |
| あ (exception: う → わ) | ない | negative |
| あ | れる (られる for ichidan) | receptive/passive — "get verbed" |
| あ | せる (させる for ichidan) | causative — allow/make to do the verb |
| え | る (られる for ichidan) | potential — "you CAN do verb" |
| お | う (よう for ichidan) | volitional — "let's do verb" |
