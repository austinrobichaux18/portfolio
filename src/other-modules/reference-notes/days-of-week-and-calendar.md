### Mnemonics

**Sunday — 日曜日 — "A Sun Day"**
Literally a sun day.

**Monday — 月曜日 — "The Moon Follows the Sun"**
月曜日 (getsuyoubi) starts with 月 (getsu/tsuki among other readings), meaning
"moon". Imagine Monday morning after Sunday night, just like the moon does
when the sun goes down.

**Tuesday — 火曜日 — "A Fiery Day"**
The 火 in 火曜日 (kayoubi) means "fire" — also associated with the planet Mars,
火星 (kasei).

**Wednesday — 水曜日 — "The Wave's Crest"**
水曜日 (suiyoubi) uses 水 (sui/mizu), meaning "water". Wednesday could be the
cool drink of water you need after Tuesday's fire — or picture the crest of a
wave to remember "suiyoubi".

**Thursday — 木曜日 — "A Tree in the Week"**
木 (moku) means "wood"/"tree". Imagine the water from Wednesday nourished a
tree that, by Thursday, had grown into a magnificent one.

**Friday — 金曜日 — "A Golden Day Before the Weekend"**
Fridays are the best — the weekend's adventure is approaching. Perhaps that's
why the first character of kinyoubi is 金, "gold"/"money".

**Saturday — 土曜日 — "The Earth Is Yours"**
It's Saturday at last! The first kanji, 土 (do), means "soil"/"earth".
