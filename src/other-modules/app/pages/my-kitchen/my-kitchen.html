How it works

Everything stays on your computer — nothing is uploaded. Each recipe gets its own subfolder with a recipe.json file and its photos. An empty folder is fine; My Kitchen fills it in as you add recipes.

Tip: keep the folder inside OneDrive (or a git repo) for free backups and history. Deleted recipes go to a _trash folder rather than disappearing.
