Data Source

All figures are the May 2025 national, cross-industry estimates from the BLS Occupational Employment and Wage Statistics (OEWS) program, downloaded directly from the BLS OEWS data tables page ("All data" file). Every column below is a field BLS publishes directly — nothing on this page is modeled, estimated, or inferred.
