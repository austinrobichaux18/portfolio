US Career Data

See how much different jobs pay, how much schooling they need, and whether they're growing or shrinking — using real U.S. government data for about 830 jobs.

Real Government Data

Pay and how many people have each job come from the government's official wage survey (Bureau of Labor Statistics, Occupational Employment and Wage Statistics), using data from May 2025. See the full BLS data tables page.

How much schooling is usually needed, whether work experience is required, and whether a job is growing or shrinking come from a separate government report ( BLS Employment Projections) that looks ahead to the year 2035. See the Employment Projections data tables page. "Job Outlook" is our own plain-language label for the government's real growth number — hover any Job Outlook cell to see the exact number.

Data pulled and last updated 10/2026.

Our Own Best Guesses (Not Official Data)

The government doesn't track everything. For 3 things — how physical the job is, how easily a computer could do it, and whether you could work from home — we made our own simple, best-guess label. These are not official numbers. We guessed based on the job's title and category (for example, a job with "mechanic" in the title gets guessed as hands-on, physical work). Treat these as a rough guide, not a fact.
