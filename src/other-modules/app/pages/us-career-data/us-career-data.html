US Career Data

Browse national employment and wage figures, real BLS education/experience/outlook data, and this site's own job-environment, AI-exposure, and remote-work estimates for ~830 US occupations.

Real BLS Data

Wages, total employment, and the 10th/90th percentile wage range are the May 2025 national, cross-industry estimates from the BLS Occupational Employment and Wage Statistics (OEWS) program, from the BLS OEWS data tables page ("All data" file).

Typical education needed, work experience required, on-the-job training, projected employment change, and annual job openings are from the 2025–35 BLS Employment Projections program ("National Employment Matrix," Table 1.2), from the BLS Employment Projections data tables page. "Job Outlook" is this site's own tier label applied to BLS's real percent-change figure (see glossary) — the percent-change and openings numbers themselves are BLS's.

Data pulled and last updated 10/2026.

Site Estimates (Not BLS Data)

Job Environment, AI Exposure, and Remote-Work Potential have no single authoritative BLS table to draw from. These three columns are this site's own simple, rule-based categorization — a baseline per SOC major group, adjusted by keyword matches on the job title (e.g. titles containing "mechanic," "nurse," or "data entry" get adjusted toward their typical environment). They're a rough general-orientation estimate, not a scientific index, and not sourced from BLS, O*NET, or any named study.
