What goes in the folder?

One subfolder per show. Episode .mp4 files can sit directly inside the show's folder, or any number of levels deeper in subfolders — for example root/precure/ep1.mp4, or root/pokemon/season1/ep1.mp4 and root/pokemon/movies/movie1.mp4 side by side. You can browse into and back out of these subfolders from the show's page.

A file named history.json is reserved at the top level of each show's folder — it's created and updated automatically to store watch progress for every episode in that show, including ones in subfolders.
